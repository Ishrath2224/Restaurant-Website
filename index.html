Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Cooked with Love

Saffron Table was born from a single conviction: that authentic Indian cuisine doesn't need shortcuts. Every broth is simmered overnight. Every spice blend is ground fresh each morning.

Our head chef, Arvind Nair, trained in Lucknow and Chettinad before bringing his craft to Hyderabad. Our menu reads like a map of the subcontinent — a different story in every dish.
