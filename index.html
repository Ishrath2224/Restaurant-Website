Our Story

Tradition Meets Modern Gastronomy

Founded in 2018, Le Velour was born out of a shared passion for bold flavors, elegant presentation, and an unforgettable dining atmosphere. Our master chefs combine time-tested culinary techniques with modern innovation to create dishes that delight every sense.

Every ingredient is locally sourced and sustainably harvested to guarantee peak freshness and quality on your plate.
